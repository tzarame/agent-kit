---
name: design
description: How a thing looks - layout, type, colour, spacing, images, brand. Use when making or changing the look of a page, a screen, a slide or a picture.
---

First learn who it is for, what it is for, and what already exists: a brand, a design system, references.
Choose one direction before you build, and hold it: one type scale, a few colours, one step of spacing.
Judge the rendered result, not the code: look at a screenshot (`shot PAGE` makes them; `--dark` for the dark theme), at phone width too, in the light and the dark theme. Fix what looks wrong.

You can overwrite the user's design files, images and tokens when no copy exists. A published page is public and stays in caches after it is deleted. Another company's brand, images or fonts used without the right bring the user trouble.
Keep the originals. Ask before you publish. Use only the assets the user has the right to use.
