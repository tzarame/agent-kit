name: ui-ux
description: How an interface works - flows, navigation, forms, states, errors, the words on the screen, accessibility. Use when designing or changing what a person does on a screen, step by step.
---

Start from what the person wants to get done, and make the path to it the shortest.
Design every state, not only the full one: empty, loading, error, success, no access.
The words on the screen say what will happen. Everything works from the keyboard, with enough contrast, a visible focus, and targets large enough for a finger.

An interface can hurt the person who uses it: a delete with no confirmation and no undo, a form that loses what was typed, a pattern that tricks the person into paying or agreeing.
Make a destructive action hard to do by mistake and possible to undo. Never design to deceive.
